# Aivora Product Documentation

## 1. Product Vision & Philosophy
Aivora is an open-source **Knowledge Operating System for the AI Era**. It transforms how software engineers, researchers, students, and technical leaders understand, evaluate, and implement artificial intelligence.

### The Five Principles
1. **UNDERSTAND**: Deconstruct AI technologies from first principles rather than repeating marketing claims.
2. **CONNECT**: Model AI as an interdependent graph ecosystem rather than isolated products.
3. **EXPLORE**: Enable discovery across foundation models, tool runtimes, and engineering frameworks.
4. **CHOOSE**: Provide objective trade-off matrices to select architectures tailored to specific privacy, budget, and latency constraints.
5. **BUILD**: Bridge theoretical knowledge into executable, sandboxed production systems.

---

## 2. Target User Personas

1. **Software Engineers & Developers**: Moving from conventional software into AI engineering; need to understand RAG, tool-calling agents, and structured schema decoding.
2. **Technical Architects & Founders**: Evaluating build-vs-buy decisions, API vs local execution models, and privacy compliance requirements.
3. **Students & Curious Beginners**: Looking for structured, hype-free learning paths starting at mathematical intuition (Level Zero).
4. **AI/ML Practitioners & Researchers**: Tracking state-of-the-art reasoning architectures, inference benchmarks, and adversarial red-teaming vectors.

---

## 3. The Core Product Loop

```
DISCOVER (AI Map / Search / Explorer)
   ↓
UNDERSTAND (First principles & 6 Knowledge Layers)
   ↓
CONNECT (Inter-node relationships & dependencies)
   ↓
CHOOSE (Find Your Stack & trade-off analysis)
   ↓
BUILD (Project Lab blueprints & sandboxed code)
   ↓
LEARN (Curriculum progression & checklists)
   ↓
RETURN (Stay updated with verified primary sources)
```

---

## 4. Competitive Differentiation
- **No Chatbot Wrapper**: Aivora is an educational and architectural platform, not another ChatGPT wrapper.
- **No Affiliate Farm**: Tools are classified strictly by **Job-to-be-Done** rather than paid sponsorship.
- **Source-First**: Every volatile factual assertion cites primary papers or official code repositories.
- **Zero Account Wall**: Full utility without accounts, cookies, or paywalls.
